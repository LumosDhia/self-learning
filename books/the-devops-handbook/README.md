### Part I Notes: The Three Ways
- **The First Way (Flow):** Optimizing the flow of work from Development to Operations to deliver value to customers quickly.
- **The Second Way (Feedback):** Creating a fast, constant flow of feedback from right to left (Ops to Dev) to fix issues early and prevent them in the future.
- **The Third Way (Continual Learning & Experimentation):** Fostering a high-trust culture that encourages risk-taking, experimentation, and learning from failure.
